## Scope and status

**COMPLETED after score and shortlist freezes.** Thirty frozen-shortlist panels
were reviewed. Every panel uses the W1 crop, its parcel overlay, and the same
target pool/context references. This review does not change retrieval scores.

## Review standard

Support requires a conjunction of the target's compact rectilinear pool,
near-building pool/patio relation, limited open turf, and substantial
contemporary flat/parapet-like massing. A single blue feature, broad
rectilinear building, or large paved access area is insufficient. `CONFLICT`
means the colour/shape detector did not establish the specified pool component,
not that a pool is absent.
