Only listings explicitly supplied after this freeze and demonstrably absent from the G5, G6, and G7 samples may enter the harness. Do not retrieve, scrape, or run the five-listing sample.

Before parcel identity recovery, freeze candidate order and its SHA-256 in a ranking file. Capture all required per-parcel artifacts and result channels without inspecting ground truth or using G5 labels. Do not evaluate, calculate metrics, select an approach, tune, or change a threshold until every selected parcel manifest is frozen. Evaluation must reference the frozen manifest hashes, retain unknown/not-runnable outcomes, and report any missing component as not runnable rather than reconstructing it.
