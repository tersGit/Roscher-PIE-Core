## Failure modes to distinguish

1. **Extraction:** a contour or its companion house/garage/road object is
   absent or fragmented.
2. **Wrong object:** the selected blue/blob-like region is not the pool, or
   the candidate pool belongs to another spatial context.
3. **Permissive metrics:** a generic metric accepts materially different
   outlines as similar.
4. **Normalization/PCA:** rotation, scale, centering, or PCA alignment
   removes the frontage/house orientation that distinguishes the layouts.
5. **Generic shape weighting:** high pool-shape contribution overwhelms
   absent property/neighbourhood contradictions.
6. **Missing spatial relation:** pool shape is compared without its relation
   to house, driveway, road, garage, and neighbouring structures.

The human observation that pool shape can visibly contradict a candidate is
therefore compatible with a high frozen Shape v2 contribution: the current
signal is pool-score-centric, not a complete property-layout fingerprint.
No water-colour feature is permitted.
