Positioned in the highly sought-after Gate 6 of Waterfall Country Estate, this exceptional residence occupies one of the estate’s most beautiful greenbelt positions. Overlooking the dam and framed by mature trees that create a remarkable sense of privacy, the home has an unmistakably calm, almost resort-like atmosphere, a private sanctuary where contemporary luxury and nature exist in perfect harmony.

From the moment you arrive, the home sets the tone. A striking entrance unfolds across a wooden deck alongside a tranquil water feature, drawing you towards the front door. Step inside and your eye is immediately carried through the home towards the greenery beyond, a view that becomes the backdrop to everyday living.

To the left of the entrance, a passage leads to an elegant formal dining room, finished with a statement chandelier and opening onto its own deck. A private study, with doors that can be closed off from the rest of the home as well as direct access outdoors, creates a peaceful and beautifully connected workspace.

The formal lounge offers an intimate setting, complete with a fireplace, while the heart of the home opens into an expansive contemporary living and entertaining space.

The open-plan kitchen is designed for both family living and effortless entertaining, complemented by a separate scullery, gas hob, oven, microwave and additional oven.

Large stacking doors dissolve the boundaries between inside and out, opening onto a substantial covered patio and the magnificent garden beyond. A further TV lounge and bar area creates an exceptional entertainment space, again opening completely through stacking doors to the outdoors.

The entertainment area has been designed almost as an outdoor kitchen and living room, complete with a built-in braai, sink, stove, generous seating and a gas fireplace, making it a space to enjoy throughout the year.

The garden enjoys a truly special position directly against the greenbelt and dam, with established trees forming a natural privacy screen and creating a feeling of complete tranquillity. A pergola provides another beautiful place to unwind, while the swimming pool offers both solar heating and a dedicated heat pump, together with blinds to cover the pool for shade.

A jungle gym provides space for children to play, while a private gate from the garden leads directly onto the greenbelt, perfect for peaceful walks through the estate without ever needing to get into the car.

Also positioned on the ground floor is a beautifully private guest suite, complete with an en-suite shower, air-conditioning and its own covered patio. A guest cloakroom, mudroom and additional storage complete the lower level.

The upper level continues the home's relaxed, luxurious character.

A generous pajama lounge with a wood-burning fireplace creates a cosy private living area, with space to incorporate a kitchenette if desired.

Bedrooms two and three each enjoy their own en-suite shower bathrooms, offering privacy and comfort for family or guests.

The fourth bedroom forms the magnificent main suite, positioned to take full advantage of the greenbelt views. Opening onto a private balcony and fitted with air-conditioning, it is a serene retreat overlooking the natural landscape.

The suite is completed by a walk-in dressing room and an impressive en-suite bathroom featuring an exceptionally large shower and separate toilet.

Beyond its beautiful design, the home has been exceptionally well equipped for modern South African living.

Double-glazed windows and doors enhance insulation, comfort and energy efficiency, while central air-conditioning, controlled separately upstairs and downstairs, keeps the home comfortable throughout the seasons.

The extensive backup power system includes 28 solar panels, a 15kW inverter and three-phase battery backup, providing substantial energy independence.

A 5,000-litre municipal backup water system adds further peace of mind.

Three garages offer direct access into the home together with additional storage, while a separate storeroom and dedicated garden-tool storage ensure that practical spaces remain neatly tucked away.

Staff accommodation includes a bedroom and bathroom facilities, with an additional separate shower bathroom.

This is far more than a beautifully designed home. It is a property defined by its position, tranquillity and connection to nature.

With the dam and greenbelt as its outlook, mature trees creating privacy, fireplaces warming both indoor and outdoor living spaces, and expansive stacking doors allowing the home to open almost entirely to its surroundings, there is a wonderful sense of calm throughout.

A rare Zen-inspired sanctuary in one of Waterfall Country Estate’s most exceptional greenbelt settings.
