Tenants in place until 2027.

Nestled within the prestigious Waterfall Country Estate, Gate 2, this exquisite residence epitomizes luxury living at its finest. As you step through the grand entrance hall, you are welcomed into a world of unmatched elegance and sophistication. The heart of this home features a cozy TV lounge, a dining room adorned with a wood-burning fireplace for intimate gatherings, and an entertainment room equipped with a built-in braai, stack doors, and a sink, perfect for hosting lavish parties. Movie enthusiasts will revel in the state-of-the-art cinema room.

The culinary space is a chef's dream, boasting an open-plan kitchen outfitted with Siemens appliances, including a gas stove, microwave, oven, and integrated fridge-freezer. A dedicated coffee room adds a touch of luxury for espresso lovers, while a separate scullery and laundry room ensure functionality meets style. Accommodation on the ground floor includes a bedroom and a separate bathroom with a shower, alongside a guest loo, leading out to a meticulously landscaped garden featuring a sparkling pool and a heated jacuzzi, offering an oasis of tranquility.

Ascending to the upper level, a linen closet adds a practical touch, while two lavish bedrooms, each with their own balconies, built-in closets, and en-suite bathrooms, offer serene retreats. The third bedroom, a masterpiece of design, features a balcony, built-in closets, a dressing table, and a full en-suite bathroom. The comfort continues with a pajama lounge, a versatile gym room/study, and the pièce de résistance - a massive main bedroom with a full bathroom and an expansive walk-in closet. An additional bedroom with a walk-in closet, full en-suite bathroom, and balcony completes this level.

Vehicle storage is abundant with two garages providing direct access into the house, plus a single garage perfect for a golf cart. This home is also equipped with solar power, backup water, double-glazed windows, and doors for enhanced comfort and efficiency. The inclusion of a staff room with a kitchen and separate bathroom ensures convenience for all.

This remarkable property, where every detail has been meticulously crafted for luxurious living, awaits its discerning new owner.
