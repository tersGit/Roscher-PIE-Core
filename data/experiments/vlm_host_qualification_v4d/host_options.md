| Cloud/on-demand | Select 16-24 GiB VRAM and 32+ GiB system RAM | Containers with Transformers/vLLM or llama.cpp | Medium-high, billing and data governance | External processing; unsuitable unless local is infeasible and an approved isolated environment exists | Fast but not the first choice for this neutral-local qualification. |

**Hardware derivation:** MINIMUM is 24 GiB RAM, 8-core/16-thread x86-64 CPU, 20 GiB free model/cache storage, Windows/Linux, and a maintained vision runtime running a quantized 7B VLM with its vision projector. PREFERRED is 32 GiB RAM, 8+ performance cores, 60 GiB free SSD, and an NVIDIA GPU with 16 GiB VRAM (12 GiB is workable at constrained context/resolution). Keep the neutral package and model cache on local encrypted storage.
