# Stronger VLM shortlist

All candidates are actual vision-language models, not text-only GGUFs. Size/RAM figures are deployment planning estimates: pin the precise artifact digest and record the observed resident memory in the portable result.

| Candidate and exact first artifact | Parameters / quantization / files | Runtime and image behavior | Planning resource requirement | Visual-spatial rationale and limits | Expected speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct**; `ggml-org/Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct-GGUF` Q4_K_M | 7B; approximately 4.5-5.5 GiB main Q4 artifact plus matching mmproj (size varies by release) | llama.cpp supports this family; use matching mmproj. Native Transformers supports interleaved multi-image messages and dynamic resolution. | 24 GiB RAM CPU minimum; 32 GiB preferred. GPU: 12 GiB workable, 16 GiB preferred. Reserve 12 GiB disk. | Stronger scale than the V4C 3B baseline and upstream reports chart, graphic, layout, localization, and JSON-coordinate capability. Its model-card HallBench result shows hallucination is not eliminated; score unknown/restraint explicitly. | CPU: several minutes/prompt on current-class mobile CPU; GPU: usually seconds to tens of seconds at constrained context. |
| **openbmb/MiniCPM-V-4_5-int4**; alternatively matching `openbmb/MiniCPM-V-4_5-gguf` release | 8B; official int4/GGUF variants; obtain the main quantized model and its matched vision/projector components exactly as documented by the release | Official card links CPU llama.cpp/Ollama support, image/video input, and 1.8 MP high-resolution images. Multi-image/video semantics require the runtime-specific release instructions. | 24 GiB RAM CPU minimum; 32 GiB preferred. GPU: 12 GiB workable, 16 GiB preferred. Reserve 12-16 GiB disk. | Official card reports high-resolution arbitrary-aspect-ratio handling and stronger MMHal-Bench behavior. Its 8B scale and vision-specific training make it a credible stronger alternative; custom/forked runtime support is a reproducibility risk. | CPU: slower than Qwen 7B at high resolution; GPU: practical for repeated runs. |
| **google/gemma-3-12b-it**; `ggml-org/gemma-3-12b-it-GGUF` Q4_K_M plus matching mmproj | 12B; approximately 7-8 GiB Q4 main artifact plus projector; exact selected quantization determines final total | llama.cpp upstream explicitly lists Gemma 3 12B; Transformers supports it from 4.50. Image inputs are normalized to 896x896 and use 256 tokens/image; multi-image fits its 128K context. | 32 GiB RAM CPU minimum; 48 GiB preferred. GPU: 16 GiB minimum, 24 GiB preferred. Reserve 20 GiB disk. | Larger model and documented image-reasoning use make it a stronger high-quality candidate. Normalization can lose tiny layout detail; do not assume a pass without the neutral run. | CPU: generally too slow for iterative work; 16-24 GiB GPU recommended. |

**First run:** Qwen2.5-VL-7B Q4_K_M in llama.cpp. It is clearly stronger than the V4C Qwen2.5-VL-3B, is explicitly listed in current upstream llama.cpp multimodal support, and has lower integration risk than MiniCPM-V 4.5 or a 12B model.

Sources: [Qwen2.5-VL 7B model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct), [MiniCPM-V 4.5 model card](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM-V-4_5), [Gemma 3 12B model card](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-3-12b-it), and [llama.cpp multimodal matrix](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/docs/multimodal.md).
